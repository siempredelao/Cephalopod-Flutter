# Privacy policy

**Cephalopod** (the Android app `gc.david.cephalopod`)

Last updated: 14 November 2026

**In short: Cephalopod collects no personal information. Nothing you do in the
app leaves your phone.**

## What the app keeps on your phone

To let you leave a game and continue it later, the app saves a few things in its
own private storage on your phone:

- the game in progress and its moves,
- your settings, such as the theme and whether sounds are on,
- whether you have seen the "How to play" screen,
- how many games you have finished (so the app knows when to ask for a rating).

This stays on your phone. The app does not send it anywhere, and the developer
cannot see it. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage in
Android's settings, or by uninstalling the app.

## What the app does not do

- It has no account, no sign-in and no ads.
- It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos or device
  identifiers.
- It has no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting.
- It does not need an internet connection, and it asks for no special
  permissions.

## Links that leave the app

Some buttons open another app or website, only when you press them:

- **How to play** and **About** link to the rule sheet and the website of Mark
  Steere, who invented the game ([marksteeregames.com](https://www.marksteeregames.com)).
- **Rate the app** opens the app's page on Google Play.

Those sites and Google Play have their own privacy policies. The app passes
them no information about you.

## Rating the app

After you finish a few games, the app may ask for a rating with Google Play's
own rating window. Google runs that window. The app does not see your rating,
your Google account or anything you type there. Google's
[privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies to it.

## Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google Play may give the developer
anonymous, summed-up statistics, such as how many people installed the app or how
often it crashed, from people who allowed that in their phone's settings. These
numbers cannot be traced back to you, and they come from Google, not from the
app.

## Children

Cephalopod is a game for everyone from about 6 years old, so children are
welcome to play it.

The app collects no personal information from anyone, children included. It has
no accounts, no ads, no chat and no purchases, and nothing a child does in the
app leaves the phone.

A few buttons open another app or website, only when pressed (see "Links that
leave the app" above). A parent may want to know that those links exist.

A parent can remove everything the app has saved by clearing its storage in
Android's settings or by uninstalling the app.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts to collect or send data, this page will change first, and
the date above will change with it. Every past version stays in the
[history of this file](https://github.com/siempredelao/Cephalopod-Flutter/commits/main/docs/privacy-policy.md).
