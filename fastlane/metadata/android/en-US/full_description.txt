Cephalopod is a short, easy-to-learn strategy game for two players, played with dice on a 5×5 board. Place a die, add up its neighbours and capture them.

HOW IT WORKS
• Take turns placing one die on an empty square.
• If two or more neighbouring dice add up to 6 or less, you capture them, and your new die shows their sum.
• Otherwise your die shows 1.
• When the board is full, the player with more dice wins. There are no draws.

FEATURES
• Play a friend on one device, or the computer at three levels: Easy, Medium and Hard
• A short step-by-step tutorial, and a hint when you are stuck
• Undo and redo
• Leave a game and continue later
• Light and dark themes, and sound effects you can turn off
• Screen reader support
• No ads, no account and no internet needed
• Available in 12 languages

ABOUT THE GAME
Cephalopod was invented by Mark Steere in 2006. This independent app keeps the game's name and rules unchanged, as Mark Steere asks. More games by Mark Steere: www.marksteeregames.com
